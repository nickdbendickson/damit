description=Multi-jurisdiction dam hazard screening (Processing provider damit_screen): preflight, a scenario-driven screening chain (inventory prepare, terrain, storage, breach direction, hydrograph, triage, 2D screening flood, preliminary suggested class), and hand-off packages for DamIT Runner. The dam inventory is the USACE National Inventory of Dams (NID): each dam needs its NID ID. Presets and thresholds come from DamIT Catalog.
about=Generalized from the DamSafety Screening workflow for the DamIT presets (FEMA 333, USBR ACER TM-11, BIA, NRCS, North Dakota REG_05.2026, Nebraska, New York, Virginia, Texas, Idaho, Colorado, Rhode Island, Arizona, California, Alaska) or custom method selection. Tools: damit_screen:preflight, damit_screen:runchain, damit_screen:prepare, :terrain, :storage, :breachdirection, :hydrograph, :triage, :engine2d (the DamSafety Screening tool-7 2D flood with the built-in finite-volume engine in this process, or LISFLOOD-FP through DamIT Runner's launcher; larger clips downloaded fresh from the USGS 3DEP service; writes Screening Depth / Velocity / DV (Max) and samples the receptors so :suggest gives a preliminary class), :suggest, :handoff (writes damit_handoff.json for damit_run:rundam) and :report. It never starts ANUGA, HEC-RAS or Wine; LISFLOOD-FP only with engine2d = lisflood (the built-in engine, shipped with DamIT Runner, is the default). Results are suggested classifications only; the engineer of record and the regulating agency make the determination. Classification of finished rasters is DamIT Hazard's job (damit:classify).

    0.3.1 - (2026-11-12) a dam line that is a MultiLineString is read through DamIT Runner 0.3.2's dam folder reader (DWR-2645), and the screening flood's notes say which part was used and why. Needs DamIT Runner 0.3.2 for the note; with an older Runner the line is skipped as before.
    0.3.0 - engine2d = lisflood runs the screening flood on LISFLOOD-FP (ACC, the same injection cells as point sources, under the screening time limit; lisflood_exe, else an installed LISFLOOD-FP, else the built-in engine with a warning); the built-in engine stays the default. larger_clips 1 (the default) now downloads each larger clip fresh from the USGS 3DEP service (as DamSafety Screening's "USGS 3DEP service" choice) into the dam folder's screen_inputs/terrain_3dep/ instead of re-reading the same terrain; a failed download is noted and that clip falls back to the run's DEM. The engine used, why, and the terrain of each clip are in the engine2d step. DAMIT_SCREENING_TIMEOUT_S is kept. DWR-2801 real-engine fixes: engine2d reports OK after a successful flood (GAP only for a partial or failed one; reaching the terrain edge is said in the detail) and its built-in engine is labelled 'DamIT Screener built-in 2D engine'; 'no terrain beyond the clip' is only concluded after a larger clip (0.2.0 also concluded it after a corridor widening and stopped at 1,500 m); breach_direction estimates the azimuth as DamSafety Screening does (dam line + reservoir point + DEM; else reservoir to dam point; else the steepest DEM drop) instead of a gap; the terrain step checks the dam point in the DEM's own CRS, so an EPSG:5070 VRT no longer says 'CRS differs or unknown'.
    0.2.0 - engine2d runs the DamSafety Screening 2D flood (built-in engine from DamIT Runner's flood package): Froehlich piping or the dam's CSV hydrograph from the top of dam, toe inflow searched 5 m to H x (DS slope + 1) (3:1 default, 150 m cap, side check), larger clips, partial maxima kept on the time limit (0.8 x DAMIT_SCREENING_TIMEOUT_S), screening rasters in the dam folder, receptor depth / velocity / DV and TM-11 M1 chart zone so suggest gives a preliminary class. Now needs DamIT Runner.
