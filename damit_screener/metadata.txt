description=Multi-jurisdiction dam hazard screening (Processing provider damit_screen): preflight, a scenario-driven screening chain (inventory prepare, terrain, storage, breach direction, hydrograph, triage, 2D screening flood, preliminary suggested class), and hand-off packages for DamIT Runner. The dam inventory is the USACE National Inventory of Dams (NID): each dam needs its NID ID. Presets and thresholds come from DamIT Catalog.
about=Generalized from the DamSafety Screening workflow for the DamIT presets (FEMA 333, USBR ACER TM-11, BIA, NRCS, North Dakota REG_05.2026, Nebraska, New York, Virginia, Texas, Idaho, Colorado, Rhode Island, Arizona, California, Alaska) or custom method selection. Tools: damit_screen:preflight, damit_screen:runchain, damit_screen:prepare, :terrain, :storage, :breachdirection, :hydrograph, :triage, :engine2d (the DamSafety Screening tool-7 2D flood with the built-in finite-volume engine, run in this process; writes Screening Depth / Velocity / DV (Max) and samples the receptors so :suggest gives a preliminary class), :suggest, :handoff (writes damit_handoff.json for damit_run:rundam) and :report. It never starts LISFLOOD-FP, ANUGA, HEC-RAS or Wine (the screening engine is Python, shipped with DamIT Runner). Results are suggested classifications only; the engineer of record and the regulating agency make the determination. Classification of finished rasters is DamIT Hazard's job (damit:classify).
