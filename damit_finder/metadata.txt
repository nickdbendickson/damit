about=Three Processing tools (Processing Toolbox > DamIT Finder > Find structures). Fills across streams: walks the 3DHP / NHD flowlines over a bare-earth DEM and reports every fill that blocks the valley (spill level, height, head, storage, pool), as a dam, dry embankment or road fill. Ponds and dugouts: classes every polygon of a water-body layer from the LiDAR. Crossings: every road / rail x stream crossing, the places a culvert or bridge must be. Every candidate is checked against the known-structure layers you give (NID, state lists, NBI, DOT culverts, USGS / SARP crossings): listed, unsure or new. Runs no flood model. Candidates for review against an aerial image, not determinations; a new dam has no NID ID, so DamIT Screener does not take it as a dam record until it is listed.
