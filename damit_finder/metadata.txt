description=Finds dams, dry dams, road fills, ponds, dugouts and road / rail stream crossings in LiDAR, hydrography and aerial imagery that are not on the NID, state or bridge / culvert lists you give.
about=Six Processing tools (Processing Toolbox > DamIT Finder > Find structures). Fills across streams and draws: walks the 3DHP / NHD flowlines and every draw traced from the DEM itself over a bare-earth DEM and reports every fill that blocks the valley (spill level, height, head, storage, pool), as a dam, dry embankment or road fill. Ponds and dugouts: classes every polygon of a water-body layer from the LiDAR. Crossings: every road / rail x stream crossing, the places a culvert or bridge must be. Open water: pond outlines from 4-band or colour-infrared aerial imagery (NDWI; several years keep the persistent water), marked when no water-body layer has them. Also: where each pool spills, dug pits and raised ridges (levees, dikes) anywhere in the DEM, the drainage area and expected pipe size at each crossing, dams / dikes / culverts from OpenStreetMap, and a score learned from the fills already on a dam list. Every candidate is checked against the known-structure layers you give (NID, state lists, NBI, DOT culverts, USGS / SARP crossings): listed, unsure or new. Runs no flood model. Candidates for review against an aerial image, not determinations; a new dam has no NID ID, so DamIT Screener does not take it as a dam record until it is listed.
