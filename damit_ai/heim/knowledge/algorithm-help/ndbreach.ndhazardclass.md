ND hazard classification (Section 5.2, ACER TM 11 charts) (ndbreach:ndhazardclass)

----------------
Description
----------------
Classifies the dam Low / Medium / High from what its breach flood reaches, following the ND Dam Safety Standards Policy REG_05.2026 Sections 5.2 and 5.3 as core/nd_hazard.py applies them (the same module as the ND dam screening plugin). Reads 'Depth (Max).tif' (ft) and 'Velocity (Max).tif' (ft/s) from the dam folder.

Habitable structures (5.2.1.1; unknown or blank occupancy counts as habitable): 2D model: potentially lethal by the ACER TM 11 depth-velocity charts (ND Fig. 5.1 houses, 5.2 mobile homes) -> High; a habitable structure in the judgment zone is Medium by rule (the old option 'judgment zone counts as potentially lethal' makes it High, 5.3). 1D model: deeper than 2 ft -> High. Inundated at any depth -> at least Medium (economic loss). Roads (5.2.1.2): AADT > 2,000 or interstate -> High, US / State highway (5.2.1.2(b), any AADT) or 500-2,000 -> Medium, private or < 500 (local / county roads) -> Low, sole access to an occupied location -> Medium. Railroads (5.2.1.3): Medium, 10 or more trains a day or passenger -> High. Lifeline facilities (5.2.2) inundated -> Medium (access roads are not traced). A downstream dam the flood reaches (depth at its point at least the wet depth) passes its class on (5.3). 'Any depth' means the wet depth (default 0.1 ft). Missing AADT or train counts are data gaps, classified by the assumption you choose.

READ FIRST. The chart method needs digitized and checked charts (core/acer_tm11_charts.json or your own chart file); the file shipped is not digitized, so the chart method stops with a message until it is. The result is a screening classification for the engineer; the official classification is made by the ND Department of Water Resources.

Structures (3.16.9, matching the DamSafety Hazard plugin's sampling footprint): a building outline is read over the cells whose centre lies in the outline or within its 5 ft edge ring; a point structure over the cells within 25 ft (20 ft assumed footprint radius + the edge ring) of the point; the cell under the centroid / point when no cell centre is that close. The peak depth with the maximum velocity over the area's wet cells, even from another cell (TM 11 practice; the velocity at the deepest cell is kept for information only). Other points (lifelines, dams): the raster cell under the point; a multipoint takes its deepest point with the maximum velocity over its wet points. Other polygons: the deepest cell whose centre falls inside (the cell under the centroid when none does). Lines: sampled every half cell, the deepest sample. Roads (3.10.90, DWR-3034): the water surface over the road surface along the centreline (1 m); bridge / notch cuts, and bare-earth openings at a mapped bridge / notch, read on a deck stand-in from the approaches; culverts not masked; the depth raster where there is no road surface or water surface, and for the whole road when the road surface DEM cannot be used (see 'Road overtopping depth'). Only features inside the rasters are read.

Writes 'Hazard Classification (ND 5.2).md' and '.json', 'Hazard Classification Findings.csv' and '.geojson', and with the chart method 'ND Fig 5.1 Houses.svg' and 'ND Fig 5.2 Mobile Homes.svg' into the dam folder.
