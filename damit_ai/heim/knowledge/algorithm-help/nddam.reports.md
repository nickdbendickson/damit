10 - Reports and statewide summary (nddam:reports)

----------------
Description
----------------
Writes index.html (counts by class, the review queue and a sortable, filterable table of every dam), summary.csv, and one page per dam in dams/ with a maximum-depth map over hillshade, the impacted features, the breach hydrograph and the notes. Pages are self-contained (open offline, print to PDF). The review queue lists dams whose screened class differs from DWR's, low confidence, truncated floods, uncertain breach direction, unstable class in tool 9, cascade overtopping, triage audit mismatches and failures.
