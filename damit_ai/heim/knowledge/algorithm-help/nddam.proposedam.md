19 - Propose a dam (nddam:proposedam)

----------------
Description
----------------
Builds ONE proposed embankment (or several, one per line) from a crest line you draw, and writes the inventory, storage curve, breach point, terrain clip and dam.json that steps 5-10 and 7b, the ND Dam Breach Runner and ND Dam Hazard already use for an existing dam. Use a work folder of its own when this is the only dam: steps 1-4 then count as done. An existing step-3 layer in the same folder is not replaced.
THE SOURCE DEM IS NOT MODIFIED. The crest is held at your design elevation and the faces fall at your slopes until they meet the natural ground. Nothing is excavated. Height is the design crest minus the lowest natural ground on the line (the streambed); the breach point is that place. Storage is measured on the pre-dam LiDAR, stage by stage — there is no water surface to see through, so nothing is estimated under a pool. A rim low point below the crest, where the pool would leave the basin, is reported; the curve still goes to the design crest and the confidence is low.
Draw the crest line from abutment to abutment. The reservoir is the LEFT of the drawn direction unless you give an upstream point. Starting values (crest width 12 ft, slopes 3H:1V) are engineer choices, not standards. Every report carries the sentence "proposed — embankment inserted from the drawn line". Screening only; the official classification is the Department's. Not run in a real QGIS.
