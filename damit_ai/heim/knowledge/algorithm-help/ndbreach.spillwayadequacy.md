Spillway adequacy (pass the Inflow Design Flood?) (ndbreach:spillwayadequacy)

----------------
Description
----------------
Checks whether the dam passes its Inflow Design Flood (ND REG_05.2026). Each inflow hydrograph in the folder (nd_pmp's <out>/hydrographs/<storm_id>.csv: '#' header lines, columns read by name time_hr and flow_cfs; or plain time_hr,flow_cfs files) is routed through the reservoir WITHOUT a breach: from the starting pool (default the crest of the spillway rating, Section 9), through the spillway rating and optional outlets / gate, and over the embankment crest (broad-crested weir C = 2.6) above the top of dam. A storm passes when the peak pool is at or below the post-settlement top of dam minus the required residual freeboard (Section 8.1.1: high / medium the greater of 1 ft and the wind setup + runup; low 3 ft). The largest multiple of each inflow the dam passes is found by bisection (up to 2x). The storm with the highest pool governs.

Required IDF by hazard class (Table 6.1): High = PMF, Medium = 0.1 % AEP, Low = 1 % AEP. Give the hydrographs of that flood: all PMP storm types and durations of the ND Statewide PMP Study (with snowmelt as appropriate). Use the entire drainage area, including non-contributing areas unless their exclusion is justified (6.2); an incremental damage analysis (6.4) may lower the IDF but the dam must still pass the 24-hour, 500-year rainstorm without overtopping.

Pass / fail comes from the routed peak pool only - never from the flood scenario's 'failed' flag, which means a breach was modelled at the peak pool. Screening aid: an engineer must review the inputs and results.
