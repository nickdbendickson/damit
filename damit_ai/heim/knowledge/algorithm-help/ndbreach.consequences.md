Life-safety and damage estimate (incremental) (ndbreach:consequences)

----------------
Description
----------------
Estimates damage (structure and contents), population at risk, warning time and loss of life for the structures a dam's flood reaches, with the breach, without it (the 'Baseline …' rasters of a flood-scenario run) and the incremental difference (with minus without, never negative, structure by structure; the idea behind ND REG_05.2026 6.4, no criterion of that section is checked). Reads 'Depth (Max).tif', 'Velocity (Max).tif' and 'Arrival Time.tif' from the dam folder. Every structure attribute is optional: an unset field is matched by name (NSI-style names work), else defaults are used and listed in the notes column.

READ FIRST. The built-in depth-damage curves are GENERIC PLACEHOLDERS, not a published source: give your own curves as CSV (occupancy, depth_ft, structure_pct, contents_pct). The built-in fatality rates are APPROXIMATE DEFAULTS from memory of the Graham (1999) / Reclamation RCEM style of guidance and MUST be checked against the current published tables before submittal; give your own as CSV (severity, none, short, some, adequate: fractions of the population at risk; severity low / medium / high). The 'destroyed', severity and population-at-risk thresholds are adjustable placeholders, not standards. Warning time = arrival time minus your detection / notification lag, both counted from breach initiation. Loss of life is an expected value for screening. Structures are sampled at their point (polygons at the centroid).

Writes 'Consequences (Structures).csv', 'Consequences Summary.md' and '.json', and 'Consequences (Affected Structures).geojson' into the dam folder.
