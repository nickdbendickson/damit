Import HEC-RAS 2D results as rasters (ndbreach:hecrasimport)

----------------
Description
----------------
Reads the maximum results of a HEC-RAS 2D plan HDF (.p##.hdf) and writes GeoTIFFs 'HECRAS Depth (Max).tif', 'HECRAS Velocity (Max).tif', 'HECRAS Arrival Time.tif' and 'HECRAS WSE (Max).tif' in ft, ft/s, hours and ft. The Depth basis decides what depth is measured from (3.10.92): 'Terrain per pixel', the default, is the cell's maximum water surface minus the actual terrain at the centre of each grid cell - the Terrain you give, else the dam folder's run DEM (the fine LiDAR) when it is finer than the HEC-RAS terrain, else the HEC-RAS terrain; 'Cell minimum' (the 3.10.84 default, conservative) is the cell's maximum water surface minus the cell's minimum terrain elevation, the same on every grid cell inside it (a ditch or channel in a cell deepens the whole cell). With the cell-minimum basis the terrain gives only a comparison. The terrain is checked against the model's cell minimum elevations: one on another vertical datum or unit stops the per-pixel basis (NDBREACH_HECRAS_DATUM_CHECK=warn keeps it with a warning). 'Terrain elevation units': Detect reads the unit the Terrain records, else the Runner's record of its own HEC-RAS terrain, else assumes its CRS's linear unit and says so; or choose Feet / US survey feet / Meters. The wet area and the arrival time follow the depth of the basis chosen. The Depth basis starts at NDBREACH_HECRAS_DEPTH_BASIS (else terrain per pixel). Velocity is the per-cell maximum when the file has one, else the maximum face velocities averaged onto the cells. Arrival is the first time the depth reaches the arrival depth, from the water surface time series, counted from the 'time of breach initiation' you give (hours after the start of the HEC-RAS run). Cells are sampled by nearest cell centre onto the grid of the 'like' raster (or of the dam folder's Depth (Max).tif), else onto a grid you set, else onto one at the median cell spacing. Coordinates must be in the same system as the like raster. Needs h5py in QGIS's Python. Tick 'compare' to run the benchmark against the dam folder's rasters. The layout of HDF files varies between HEC-RAS versions and was not tested against real files: if it cannot find the datasets it lists what the file contains.
