Find road and rail crossings of streams (culverts) (ndfind:crossings)

----------------
Description
----------------
Where roads and railroads cross streams: the places a culvert or a bridge must be (Finder 0.1).<br><br>Pure Python. Every crossing of a road / rail centreline with a stream line is a crossing; the
LiDAR shows the fill but not the pipe under it. Each crossing is then checked against the
structure lists the engineer gives (NBI bridges and culverts, NDDOT bridge inventory and
centre-line pipes, USGS / SARP stream crossings; core/listed.py): a crossing with no listed
structure near it is a culvert nobody has on a list.<br><br>Segments are put in a bucket grid so a county of roads against a county of streams is not every
pair. Crossings closer than `dedupe_m` (a divided road, a line digitised twice) are one.<br><br>EXPECTED PIPE SIZE (with a DEM): the drainage area above each crossing and its longest flow path
(core/terrain.py), the Kirpich time of concentration, the rational-method peak flow Q = C i A for
YOUR runoff coefficient C and rainfall intensity i (NOAA Atlas 14 at that duration and the design
return period), and the round pipe that carries Q flowing full at YOUR velocity. An order of
magnitude for screening - the rational method is meant for small areas (about 200 acres); above
that use a regional regression (USGS StreamStats) - not a hydraulic design.<br><br>Known structures for crossings: NBI bridges (pack NBI_bridges; item 43B 19 = culvert), NDDOT bridge inventory, NDDOT centre-line pipes, USGS_stream_crossings, SARP / ABI road crossings. The Runner's culvert guesses (CULVERT_BURN_GUESSES) use the same road x stream idea per dam; this tool lists them for a county or the state.
