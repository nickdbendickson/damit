Import HEC-RAS plan results (2D, .hdf) (ndhazard:hecrasplan)

----------------
Description
----------------
Reads the 2D flow area results of a HEC-RAS 6.x plan (<project>.p##.hdf) and writes Depth_ft, Velocity_ftps, Arrival_hr, WSE_ft and TimeOfPeak_hr GeoTIFFs. With the terrain raster, depth is water surface minus terrain on the terrain grid. Without it, the Runner's current per-pixel rasters of the plan are used when there are some (2.18.61): the newest Runner re-export of the plan's current results (_hecras/reexport_*) when it says terrain (from 2.18.63 the newest decides: after a newer cell_min one no older one is used), else the dam folder's rasters when its _hecras/hecras_stage.json says terrain for this plan and they are not newer than it (a fresh terrain stage); otherwise depth is water surface minus each cell's lowest point (the cell minimum, the Runner's default). The basis used is in depth_basis.json. Arrival / time of peak are hours after the breach start (hours after simulation start). Check the first results against RAS Mapper.
