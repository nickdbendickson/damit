3 - Estimate reservoir storage (nddam:estimatestorage)

----------------
Description
----------------
For each dam, finds the nearest NHD HR waterbody, reads the pool elevation from the hydro-flattened DEM, measures storage between pool and crest directly from the DEM, and ESTIMATES storage below pool by projecting the shoreline slope inward (capped by dam height). The below-pool part is the uncertain one: on a synthetic rounded pond it overestimated by ~40%. If the dam has inventory normal storage (DWR or NID), that value is used (stor_method = inventory): it calibrates the below-pool part of the curve (below_scale) while the above-pool part stays DEM-measured. inv_vs_est is inventory normal storage / estimated storage at normal pool. Use the shape factor to calibrate against dams with known storage.

Storage to use (default Max storage): Max places the inventory maximum at the max pool - (DWR max_pool_elev - normal_elev) above the lidar water surface, which is the normal pool - with the normal storage at the normal pool and only the DEM fill above the max pool added on top (stor_anchor, stor_anchor_m). A max pool above the DEM crest is placed at the crest (MAXPOOL-ABOVE-CREST): the storage at the top of dam is set to the inventory maximum (never below it), which can be less than the normal storage plus the DEM fill to the crest (the inventory difference replaces that fill, as it does between the two pools). Without a usable normal storage, a DEM fill from the pool to the max pool larger than the maximum keeps the DEM curve (FILL-EXCEEDS-MAX; the storage at the top of dam is then above the maximum); with one, the DEM fill between the two pools is scaled to fit between them. Without the pool elevations (NID-only records), or with a max pool below the normal pool or at it with a larger maximum (MAXPOOL-ELEV-BAD), it raises the storage at the top of dam to the inventory maximum as before, flagged FALLBACK in stor_notes. A normal storage equal to the maximum (both read from the max-storage field) is flagged NORMAL-EQUALS-MAX and not used at the normal pool; one above the maximum is flagged NORMAL-NOT-BELOW-MAX (not used at the normal pool when the pool elevations are known, kept there as before in the fallback). Normal keeps the DEM-measured part above the normal pool (a sunny-day check); Larger of the two uses whichever inventory value is larger. A missing or zero value falls back to the other one (Normal without a normal storage uses the maximum, Max without a maximum uses the normal); with neither, the DEM estimate is kept. Dams without a water body use the chosen value for their assumed full-height curve. stor_notes and the log start with the storage used and any fallback.
