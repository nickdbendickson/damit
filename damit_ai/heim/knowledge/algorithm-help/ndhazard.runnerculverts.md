Culvert and bridge lines for DamSafety Runner (ndhazard:runnerculverts)

----------------
Description
----------------
Writes the lines DamSafety Runner opens through road and rail embankments ('Culverts, bridges and notches': lines, upstream end first; fields kind, span_ft, rise_ft, barrels, shape, invert_up, invert_dn, name, matched by name) from the National Bridge Inventory, OpenStreetMap crossings and, when given, NDDOT's bridge inventory (Bridge_Inventory_External) and centerline pipes (Center_Line_Pipes).
Each structure is placed where a stream crosses a road or rail line within the search distance of its point (the nearest crossing; rail first for railroad bridges). The line follows the stream, upstream end first (3DHP flowlines are digitised in the flow direction; tick 'reversed' for streams drawn upstream), and is (embankment top width + 2 x side margin) long, divided by the sine of the crossing angle (at least 30 degrees). The top width is the NBI deck / roadway width when known, else your road / rail width.
NDDOT: the bridge inventory's culvert description ('DOUBLE, 10X7X88' RCB') is read for the barrels, span x rise, barrel length and shape (box / pipe / arch); the centerline pipes give diameter (or arch span x rise), barrels, length and the elevation of each end. The two end elevations are written as the inverts: NDDOT's left and right are as seen looking from the lower station number to the higher (that direction is read from the neighbouring pipes on the route, else HWY_DIR), and the side the stream comes from is the inlet (an inlet below the outlet is noted). Their datum is not stated, so DamSafety Runner compares them with the LiDAR bed before using them (its 'inventory elevation tolerance', 3 ft to start: within it they are used, both ends off alike is corrected as a datum shift, otherwise the LiDAR inverts are used and the run report says so). A line with an inventory barrel length is that long plus the margins (the barrel already runs toe to toe).
Unknown values stay EMPTY - the NBI and OpenStreetMap give no rise, shape or inverts, the NBI span and barrels only - and the Runner then uses its own defaults (a DEM invert, its default span, a box). Each line records its source, the inventory text and the match distance. Structures that could not be placed (no stream / road crossing near, not over a waterway, a duplicate of another record) are listed in the layer 'culverts_not_placed' and a CSV beside the GeoPackage.
Layers left empty are read from the offline data pack (or downloads folder) for the area: NBI_bridges, OSM_crossings, NHD_flowlines, ND_roads_NG911 (else HPMS), NARN_rail_lines. Search distance, widths and margin are screening parameters chosen by the engineer (defaults are placeholders, not standards). Output in EPSG:5070.
