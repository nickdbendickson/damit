6 - Quick screen (triage) (nddam:triage)

----------------
Description
----------------
Takes about a second per dam. Builds a deliberately generous flood envelope from the breach peak: unattenuated peak flow, Manning normal depth in valley cross-sections along the steepest-descent path (through potholes and, with stream lines, through road fills), a safety margin, and a reach that stops only when the valley would hold 'volume factor' x the released volume.
tri_result = clear: no habitable structure (tool 8's rule: habitable unless the occupancy names an uninhabited building), no road that could rate Medium or High (only a known AADT < 500, not interstate, not a US / State highway, not sole access is left out; an unknown AADT counts), no railroad, no lifeline facility and no other dam of the layer lies inside the envelope, so the dam screens LOW without the 2D model. needs 2D: something is inside, the envelope reached the terrain edge, or the dam is above the size limit. Tool 7 skips cleared dams.
AUDIT: a random share of cleared dams is still sent to tool 7 (tri_audit = yes); tool 8 reports whether the 2D model agreed. Screening shortcut only.
