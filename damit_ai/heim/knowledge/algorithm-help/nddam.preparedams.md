1 - Prepare dam inventory (nddam:preparedams)

----------------
Description
----------------
Builds one metric dam layer. Defaults match the ND DWR 'Dams by Hazard Class' export (feet, acre-feet, acres; 0 = not recorded).
Status filter: only dams whose status is in the list are kept (default 'Intact'); breached, removed, not-built, submerged and location-inconsistent dams are dropped. Leave empty to keep all.
Size filter (optional): a dam is skipped only when BOTH its height and its storage are known and BOTH are below your limits, so dams with missing data are never skipped by accident. Use it to run the ~1000 larger dams first.
NID (optional): matched to DWR dams first by NID ID, then by distance; matched values fill gaps in the DWR record (source = DWR+NID). NID dams with no DWR dam are added (source = NID).
A blank field box is filled from the layer when the tool runs (DWR and NID names both match). NID boxes start from NIDID, NAME, DAM_HEIGHT, NORMAL_STORAGE, MAX_STORAGE, SURFACE_AREA, HAZARD_POTENTIAL, OPERATIONAL_STATUS and DAM_LENGTH when that layer is picked.
shared_vol = YES marks records that share an identical large volume (e.g. levee 'reach' segments of one reservoir): they are not separate reservoirs and need manual handling. Existing hazard text is kept as-is; DWR says 'Significant' where the ND policy says 'Medium'.
struct_cls / flag_note (0.17.26): a flagged structure of the suite's list (docs/Flagged_structures.md, e.g. DWR-3161, a road fill over backwater) is kept with its class and note; the later tools list it with the note and its hazard of record instead of a breach flood.
inv_maxa_m2 (0.17.46): the DWR maximum-pool surface area (max_pool_area, acres, recorded at the top of dam), in m2; empty when the inventory has none (0) and for NID dams. Tool 7b sends it as the Runner's surface area when the tool-3 stage-storage curve is too shallow to give one, or when no curve folder is given (0.17.49).
inv_norm_elev_m / inv_max_elev_m (0.17.47): the normal-pool and max-pool elevations (DWR normal_elev / max_pool_elev; the NID has none). Tool 3 places the maximum storage at the max pool, their difference above the lidar water surface.
inv_area_m2: the NORMAL-pool surface area (DWR normal_area / normal_are, NID SURFACE_AREA). 0.17.49: on the suite GeoPackage it held max_pool_area before; a max-pool field picked for it is used but warned in the log.
DWR dugouts (optional, 0.17.54; filled from the suite folder's dwr_dugouts and dwr_dugout_polygons): a dam inside a DWR dugout polygon or within the buffer (default 100 ft = 30 m) of a DWR dugout point is a dugout, not a dam: it is left out and listed in the log and in 1_dugouts.csv with the reason 'DWR dugout <structure_id>'. A dam DWR gives a real hazard class (High / Significant / Medium / Low; not Undetermined) or that has an NID id is never left out: it is kept and warned. Both empty = no change.
