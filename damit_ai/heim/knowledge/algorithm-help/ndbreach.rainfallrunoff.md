Rainfall to inflow (PMF) hydrograph (ndbreach:rainfallrunoff)

----------------
Description
----------------
Builds the reservoir inflow hydrograph for the flood scenario from a storm: storm depth x temporal distribution, minus losses (SCS curve number, or a constant initial loss and rate, or none), convolved with a unit hydrograph (SCS dimensionless with an adjustable peak rate factor, Clark, or the SCS shape with your own lag), plus a constant base flow (snowmelt / antecedent flow). Writes the CSV (time hr, discharge cfs) that 'PMF inflow hydrograph CSV' reads; time zero is the start of the storm.

The storm depth is YOUR input (PMP or a design storm for the chosen duration); no PMP value is generated. Use the contributing drainage area (leave out non-contributing pothole areas). The SCS Type II curve is defined for 24 hr. Time of concentration: your value, the SCS lag equation lag = l^0.8 (S+1)^0.7 / (1900 Y^0.5) hr with S = 1000/CN - 10 and tc = lag/0.6 (l ft, Y average watershed slope in %, AMC II CN), or Kirpich tc = 0.0078 L^0.77 S^-0.385 min (L ft, S ft/ft; use the main-channel slope). Both are small-basin formulas. The time step is tc/5, at most 1/96 of the storm (1-60 min, dividing the storm evenly); the hydrograph volume is checked against the excess rainfall (within 1 %) and warnings are listed for questionable inputs.
