7 - Run breach flood simulations (nddam:runbreachsim)

----------------
Description
----------------
Runs a 2D dam-break flood for every dam, several dams at once.
ENGINES: LISFLOOD-FP ACC (fastest; needs the LISFLOOD-FP program), LISFLOOD-FP FV1, the built-in engine (nothing to install; explicit finite-volume shallow-water solver like DSS-WISE, exact maximum depth x velocity), or the built-in engine with the reservoir in the grid: the reservoir starts full at the top of dam, its bed under the LiDAR water surface is estimated and calibrated to the tool-3 storage, and the breach cells are lowered over the formation time. The first three inject the tool-5 hydrograph just below the dam.
AUTOMATIC DOMAIN: cell size by reservoir size (0 = auto); only a corridor along the downstream flow path is modelled; if the flood reaches the corridor wall it is widened, if it reaches the terrain edge a larger clip is loaded (from 3DEP or the local folder when selected); if the flood is still spreading at the end, the run is extended.
ROAD CROSSINGS: with stream lines (3DHP flowlines) supplied, short road fills that block a stream are cut at the model resolution (culvert gap); DWR dam lines are never cut.
CASCADE: other DWR dams reached by the flood are listed with the depth over their crest; with 'breach', an overtopped dam that is also in this batch fails too, its own hydrograph starting when the flood reaches it.
Dams cleared by tool 6 (triage) are skipped unless picked for the audit. Resumes: dams with existing result rasters are read, not re-run.
A second set, Runner names, is written into <dam_id>/: Depth (Max).tif in feet, Velocity (Max).tif in ft/s, D x V (Max).tif in ft^2/s, Arrival Time.tif in hours. The *_depth.tif working grids stay metres.
NO-BREACH BASELINE (v0.11, off by default): screening models the breach flood only. With BASELINE on, a second run of the same domain releases only a steady ambient flow of BASELINE_CFS (a value YOU choose - the tool does not know the spillway or stream flow; 0 = dry baseline) at the breach site, with the dam intact, and writes <dam>_<scenario>_base_depth.tif (m), _base_vel.tif (m/s) and the increment _incr_depth.tif = breach depth - baseline depth (m, clipped at 0). Fields base_run, base_cfs, base_max_m, incr_max_m, incr_tif. Tool 8 can then count impacts from the increment (INCREMENTAL). This is a screening approximation of ND's flood-scenario comparison (breach vs no-breach flood); the real design-flood (PMF) routing is the Runner's / the engineer's. The baseline roughly doubles the run time of a dam.
BUILDINGS (v0.11, off by default): LiDAR bare-earth terrain has no buildings. Optionally the cells whose centre lies inside a building footprint polygon (BLD; a point layer is ignored with a message) are raised by BUILDING_RAISE_FT (default 10 ft, YOUR value; water flows around the block) or get Manning's n = BUILDING_N (default 0.15, YOUR value, times the n multiplier; water passes, slowed). Breach injection cells, the modelled reservoir and the dam are never changed. Written to sim_notes and the 'buildings' field. Screening approximation; not run in a real QGIS.
