Find open water in aerial imagery (pond outlines) (ndfind:water)

----------------
Description
----------------
Open water in aerial imagery: the pond outlines no hydrography layer has (Finder 0.1).<br><br>Pure numpy / scipy. The water index is NDWI (McFeeters 1996) from the green and near-infrared
bands of 4-band or colour-infrared imagery (NAIP, the ND statewide orthos):<br><br>    NDWI = (green - NIR) / (green + NIR)<br><br>Open water absorbs near-infrared and comes out positive; crops, grass and bare soil negative;
stubble and wet soil near 0. A cell is water when NDWI >= `threshold` (YOUR value; 0.15 is a
starting value for NAIP in late summer - check it on a known pond) and it is dark in the
near-infrared (at most `nir_frac` of the land's median: metal roofs, gravel, rail cars and
shadows can have a positive NDWI but are not that dark). The mask is opened by
`open_px` cells (drops field edges, roads and roof shadows narrower than about 2 x open_px + 1
cells) and closed again, then every connected patch of at least `min_px` cells that is somewhere
at least `min_width_px` wide is one water body (a ditch along a road or railroad is long but
narrow).<br><br>Imagery shows what was wet on the day it was flown: a dry year misses ponds, a wet year adds
flooded fields and potholes. Emergent vegetation (cattails) hides part of a marshy pool. The
outlines are for the pond tool (core/ponds.py), which then asks the LiDAR whether the water is
held above the ground.<br><br>Layers: the pack's 3DHP_waterbodies (or NHD / NWI) as the mapped water bodies. The 'water' polygons with mapped = no are the ponds no layer has: give them to 'Find ponds and dugouts' as its water bodies to ask the LiDAR whether each is held above the ground.<br><br>An image service is downloaded for the extent only (tiles are kept beside the output and reused). Every threshold is YOUR value.
