Build a class-9 water mosaic from LiDAR tiles (ndfind:water_mosaic)

----------------
Description
----------------
Point it at a folder of classified LiDAR tiles (USGS 3DEP .las/.laz) and it writes the water_count.vrt and water_elev.vrt that 'Find open water' reads with its 'Prebuilt class-9 water-count raster' input. Build it once per area; the open-water tool then reads the mosaic a tile at a time, so a county or the whole state is in reach without re-reading the raw point cloud. Tiles whose CRS is not in the file need their EPSG. Dry land and the gaps between tiles read back as no water.<br><br>Output CRS: North Dakota (UTM 14N). Two rasters are written: water_count (class-9 points per cell) and water_elev (their mean elevation, the pond water surface). Give the count VRT to 'Find open water' as its 'Prebuilt class-9 water-count raster'.
