```
4 - Breach point and flow direction (nddam:breachdirection)

----------------
Description
----------------
Matches each dam to its DWR dam line (by DWR structure ID first, then by distance and name), works out which side is downstream (away from the reservoir; terrain is the cross-check), and places the breach where the ground at the downstream toe is lowest, i.e. where the embankment is tallest. Outputs a breach point layer and a direction-arrow line layer so you can check every dam visually. dir_conf = low means the reservoir and the terrain disagreed; those dams need a manual look. Dams with no dam line fall back to 'flow away from the reservoir centroid' (dir_method = centroid, conf = low). Without cached terrain the breach goes to the line point nearest the inventory location.
```
