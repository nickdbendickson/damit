description=Suggested dam hazard class from existing flood rasters and receptor layers, by jurisdiction preset (FEMA 333, USBR, BIA, NRCS and 11 states) or by methods you pick. NID dams only: the dam record names the dam by its USACE National Inventory of Dams ID.
about=One Processing tool (damit:classify) and a window. Reads maximum depth / velocity rasters (and optional DV, water surface, non-breach and arrival rasters) of a breach run made elsewhere - it does not run any model - and scores structures, roads, rail, facilities, lifelines and environmental features with the Appendix A methods of the US Dam Hazard Classification Corrected Edition (Nov 9, 2026). Every threshold, preset and label comes from the DamIT Catalog plugin (Appendix A as data; install it first). Output: the suggested class with its driving receptors and method IDs, a receptor layer styled by class and a run report with flags and citations. Informational, not a regulatory determination: the engineer of record and the regulating agency make the determination. Based on DamSafety Hazard's sampling and TM-11 charts.
