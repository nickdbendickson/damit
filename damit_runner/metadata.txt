Tools: damit_run:rundam (prepare / record only; a request for LISFLOOD-FP, ANUGA or HEC-RAS, or DRY_RUN off, is refused with a clear message) and damit_run:checkhandoff. It never starts LISFLOOD-FP, ANUGA, HEC-RAS, Wine, ndbreach:rundam or ndbreach:batch, and always creates a new run folder rather than writing into an existing one. Classification of finished rasters is DamIT Hazard's job (damit:classify); the Runner attaches catalog metadata only. Suggested classifications only; the engineer of record and the regulating agency make the determination.
